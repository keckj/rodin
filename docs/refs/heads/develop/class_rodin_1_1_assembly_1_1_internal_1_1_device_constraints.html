DeviceConstraints

Constraints of a device system, resolved on the host.

Defined in Rodin/Kokkos/Assembly/Kokkos.h

template <
  class Backend>
class DeviceConstraints;
Template parameters
Backend Kokkos backend of the mesh context.

Value constraints are recorded as they are assembled. When an identification Dirichlet condition or a periodic condition is present, resolve() replays every condition, in order, in a host ConstraintMap, as the host assemblies do, and uploads the expansion of every degree of freedom on its masters.

template<class Backend> template<class Map, class Offset, class Finalize>
Resolved resolve(const ExecutionSpace& execution, Map& map, std::vector<Index>& globals, std::vector<unsigned char>& owned, Offset&& offset, Finalize&& finalize)

Resolves the conditions of a distributed system in the global numbering, then expands the local degrees of freedom on local indices.

Parameters
execution Execution space of the uploads.
map Empty constraint map of the global degrees of freedom.
globals Global index of each local degree of freedom, appended with the masters the process does not hold.
owned Whether the process owns each local degree of freedom, appended likewise.
offset Callable giving the global index of the first degree of freedom of a field from its local one.
finalize Callable resolving the map over the processes; collective.
Returns Resolved constraints in local indices, with empty expansions when no degree of freedom stays identified.

The periodic and identification conditions relate global indices of their spaces, which may be held by other processes; each process may give any of them, and the map gathers them all. The fixed degrees of freedom are those already synchronized over the processes. The masters the process does not hold are appended to the local degrees of freedom, so that their entries are sent to their owners, and only the owner of an identified degree of freedom writes its row.
