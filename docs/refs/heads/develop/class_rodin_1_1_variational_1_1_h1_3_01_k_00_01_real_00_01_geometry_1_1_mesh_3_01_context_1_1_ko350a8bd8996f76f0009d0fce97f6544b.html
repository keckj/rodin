H1 final

Real valued H1 Lagrange space on a resident Kokkos mesh.

The space numbers its degrees of freedom as the host space of the same degree built on Geometry::Mesh::toHost(), so the two share their global numbering. The host space is kept for the form language queries, and its numbering is copied to the device, where the basis is evaluated by Rodin::Kokkos::Variational::LagrangeDOFs.

Every geometry but the pyramid is available on the device.
