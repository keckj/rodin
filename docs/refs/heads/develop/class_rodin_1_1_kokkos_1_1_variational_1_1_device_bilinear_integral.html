DeviceBilinearIntegral

Device integration of a trial/test dot product over a region of a Kokkos mesh.

Represents

for scalar or vector device trial and test spaces on a resident Kokkos mesh, possibly different, such as the velocity and pressure spaces of a Stokes problem. The region is given by getRegion(): the cells, the faces, the boundary or the interface of the mesh. The operators and are lowered once to DeviceView, then a kernel integrates every polytope of the region with one thread and writes its local matrix as device triplets.

A face is integrated with the basis of the face, whose degrees of freedom are the ones of the face, as on the host.

The quadrature order is selected on the host as by the QuadratureRule the host would use, once per reference geometry, as described in Internal::DeviceQuadrature.

The host evaluation interface (setPolytope(), integrate()) is not available and raises; the form is assembled by Assembly::Kokkos.

Rodin::Variational::Integral, BoundaryIntegral, FaceIntegral and InterfaceIntegral specialize this class for device spaces.

The quadrature points are taken by groups of P points. The threads of the team build the integration points of a group in team scratch memory, one point per thread, then evaluate the test and trial basis functions at these points, one pair of a point and a basis function per thread. Each thread then accumulates in registers the entries of rank such that , for its rank r in a team of T threads. The local matrix is covered by blocks of TeamEntries entries per thread, or TeamWideEntries beyond LocalCapacity, so its size is not bounded. A component diagonal integrand (isComponentDiagonal()) integrates the pairs of scalar degrees of freedom only, with the payloads of the values of component 0 (StoredValue), and writes each entry on the diagonal of its component block (Internal::writeEntries()).
