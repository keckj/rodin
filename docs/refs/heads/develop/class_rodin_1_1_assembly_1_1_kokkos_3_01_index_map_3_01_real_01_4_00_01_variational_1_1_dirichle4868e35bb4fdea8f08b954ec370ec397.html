Kokkos final

Device assembly of a value Dirichlet condition on a Lagrange Kokkos space.

The constrained faces are the faces of codimension one carrying one of the essential attributes or, when no attribute is given, the boundary faces. Each constrained degree of freedom is assigned to its first face, where the value is evaluated at its node. Grid function values are therefore interpolated from the degrees of freedom of that face, which matches the host for continuous data.
