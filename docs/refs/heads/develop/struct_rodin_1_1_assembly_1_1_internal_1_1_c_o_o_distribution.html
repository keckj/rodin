COODistribution

PETSc linear system assembled in device memory, with value constraints eliminated.

Defined in Rodin/Kokkos/Assembly/PETSc.h

struct COODistribution;

The elimination is the one of DeviceSystem: rows of fixed degrees of freedom become identity rows holding the fixed value, and the columns of fixed degrees of freedom move to the right-hand side. Eliminated entries keep their coordinates with a zero value, and every diagonal entry is listed, so the pattern of the matrix does not depend on the constraints.

Integrators with the same layout between the same spaces share one segment of the coordinate list, their values being summed in place, so a sum of integrands such as the terms of an Oseen operator lists each local matrix entry once.

Identification constraints are resolved on the host, see DeviceConstraints. The coordinate list is then expanded on the masters of the rows and columns, the identified columns lifting their affine values to the right-hand side, and the identified rows become the rows of their constraints. The expanded pattern is kept as the plain one.

Distribution of the degrees of freedom of a system over MPI processes.

The local degrees of freedom, ghosts included, are those the device integrators number. Each one has a global index and is owned by one process, the one owning it in its space. The process holds the contiguous range of the rows of the PETSc layout, whose length is its number of owned degrees of freedom. With a single field this range is the owned range of the space.

With several fields, a degree of freedom is known by its index in the system as on the host, the offset of its field plus its index in the space of the field, and the constraints and the preassembled operators are resolved in this numbering. The range of the process holds its owned degrees of freedom of the first field, then those of the second one, and so on, so that the rows of a process are the degrees of freedom of its part of the mesh. getPETScIndex() maps the index in the system to the row of the PETSc layout, and getFieldStarts() gives the first row of each field on the process.
