LagrangeBasis

Lagrange basis of degree K on the device.

The basis function of node of a reference geometry is expanded in monomials of the reference coordinates,

over on the simplices, on the quadrilateral and the hexahedron, and on the wedge, so that it spans the space of Rodin::Variational::H1Element. The coefficients invert the Vandermonde matrix of the monomials at the host nodes, computed once on the host in extended precision.

The nodes of the segment, the quadrilateral and the hexahedron are the tensor products of the nodes of the segment, so their basis functions factor into the one dimensional Lagrange polynomials

where are the indices of the coordinates of node and the barycentric weights. They are evaluated in this form, which takes a fixed number of operations whatever the node.

The physical gradient follows from the reference one through , where is the P1 basis cached by IntegrationPointView and its reference vertex, the P1 basis reproducing the reference coordinates. Pyramids are not supported.
