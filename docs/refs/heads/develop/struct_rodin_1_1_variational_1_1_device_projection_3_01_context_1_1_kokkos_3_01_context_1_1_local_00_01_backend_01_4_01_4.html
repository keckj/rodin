DeviceProjection

Projection on grid functions of device spaces of a resident Kokkos mesh.

Defined in Rodin/Kokkos/Variational/Projection.h

template <
  class Backend>
struct DeviceProjection<
  Context::Kokkos< Context::Local, Backend >>;

Template parameters
Backend Kokkos backend of the mesh context.

The source is evaluated on the device at the node of every degree of freedom of the selected polytopes, as the host evaluates the linear forms of the elements. When polytopes share a degree of freedom, the one of largest index sets it, as the last one visited on the host. Degrees of freedom outside the selection keep their values.

The source is a function expression with a device lowering, a callable taking a const Rodin::Kokkos::Geometry::PointView&, or a constant. A device predicate, callable on a const Rodin::Kokkos::Geometry::PolytopeView& and returning bool, further restricts the selected polytopes.
