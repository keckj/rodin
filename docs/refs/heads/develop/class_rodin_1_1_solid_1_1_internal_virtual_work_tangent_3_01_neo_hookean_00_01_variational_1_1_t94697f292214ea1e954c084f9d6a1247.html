Represents

for vector device spaces, with the Neo-Hookean material tangent of NeoHookean::getMaterialTangent(). For the vector Lagrange bases and , with , the entry is

the contraction of the host dense tangent.

The quadrature order is that of the host, unless set by setQuadratureOrder(). The displacement is copied to the device at every assembly. Constitutive inputs are not available on the device.

The quadrature points are taken by groups of P points. The threads of the team build the integration points of a group in team scratch memory, one point per thread, then the gradients , one pair of a point and a scalar basis function per thread, which the test and state spaces share when they have the type of the trial space. They then sum the displacement gradients, one pair of a point and a basis function of the state per thread, build the kinematics, one point per thread, and the pullbacks , one pair of a point and a scalar basis function per thread. Each thread then takes a slot, a trial function and a block of TeamPairs test functions, and accumulates in registers the vdim by vdim entries of each of its pairs over the group, reusing the products of the trial function across the block. The entries are laid out as by integratePolytopes().
