ConnectivityView

Read-only device representation of the graded incidence complex.

Architecture

Entity arrays are flattened by dimension. Ordered vertex lists are stored separately from arbitrary incidence relations, preserving reference vertex orientation. Relations are independently owned CSR arrays. This view neither owns storage nor computes topology. Connectivity mutation or owner destruction invalidates every borrowed handle.
