LinearElasticityIntegratorBase

Linear elasticity integrator for vector spaces on a resident Kokkos mesh.

Assembles

on the device as the sum of two device integrals, which is the bilinear form of the host integrators. Both integrals use the quadrature order of the host, , with and the orders of the Lamé parameters, zero when they report none, and , the degrees of the trial and test elements.

The host evaluation interface (setPolytope(), integrate()) is not available and raises; the form is assembled by Assembly::Kokkos. The specializations of Rodin::Variational::LinearElasticityIntegrator for the vector P1 and H1 spaces of a resident mesh derive from this class.

Integrates both terms on the device.

Parameters
values Entry values, the entry of rank r being written at offset + getEntryPosition(r, count, getSpacing()).
offset Position of the first entry.
sign Factor of the contributions.
accumulate Whether the contributions of the dilatation term are added to the values instead of replacing them.
rows Row indices, test degrees of freedom, written as the values unless empty.
cols Column indices, trial degrees of freedom, written as the values unless empty.

The shear term accumulates onto the entries of the dilatation term, which share their arrangement.
