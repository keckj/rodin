LagrangeDOFs

Degrees of freedom of an H1 space of degree K on the device.

Defined in Rodin/Kokkos/Variational/H1.h

Holds the local to global numbering of every polytope of the mesh, copied from the host space, and the basis. Component of the scalar degree of freedom has the global index , where is the stride, the size of the scalar space.

The quadratic simplices keep the closed form of QuadraticBasis, the other polytopes take LagrangeBasis.
