Read-only borrowed device mesh with attached geometric maps.

Architecture

Topology, labels, coordinates and geometric control points refer to allocations owned by the resident mesh. Polytopes and points are small value handles into those allocations. Point evaluation uses thread-local fixed-capacity numerical objects; no host objects or virtual functions are accessed. Destruction or replacement of the owner invalidates this view and all derived handles.
