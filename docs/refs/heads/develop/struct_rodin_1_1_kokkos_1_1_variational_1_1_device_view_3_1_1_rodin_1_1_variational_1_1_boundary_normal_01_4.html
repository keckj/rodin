Outward unit normal of the boundary faces.

The normal of a face of a mesh of dimension two or three, embedded in a space of the same dimension, is computed from the face Jacobian as on the host: for an edge and the cross product of the Jacobian columns for a surface. It is oriented away from the cell incident to the face, whose vertex mean stands for its interior, then normalized. Evaluating it needs the face to cell incidence. Surface meshes embedded in three dimensions and codimension two entities are not supported.
