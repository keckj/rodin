QuadraticBasis

Quadratic Lagrange basis of the simplices.

Defined in Rodin/Kokkos/Variational/H1.h

class QuadraticBasis;

Contents

  • Reference
    • Public static variables
    • Public static functions

Node of a simplex follows the node order of Rodin::Variational::H1Element of degree two. It is either the vertex or the midpoint of the edge , and its basis function is a product of barycentric coordinates

The barycentric coordinates of a simplex are its P1 basis, so the values and physical gradients are combinations of the P1 basis cached by IntegrationPointView.
