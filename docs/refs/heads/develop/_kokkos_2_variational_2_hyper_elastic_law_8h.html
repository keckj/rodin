Device hyperelastic laws of constant parameters.

Contents

  • Reference

Each device law evaluates, at a displacement gradient and , the first Piola-Kirchhoff stress and the elasticity tensor

whose action on is the host HyperElasticLaw::getMaterialTangent(). The device law of a host law is given by DeviceLaw.
